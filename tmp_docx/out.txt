⚠ PRIORITY HAZARD ACTIONS — THIS INVENTORY

Acrylamide — IARC 2A neurotoxic carcinogen. Never weigh without gloves + fume hood; polymerised gel is safe.

Benzene, Carbon Tetrachloride, Perchloroethylene — IARC Group 1/2A carcinogens. Evaluate elimination/substitution; these are legacy solvents with safer modern alternatives.

Nickel Chloride — IARC Group 1 carcinogen. Minimise all use.

Phenyl Hydrazine Hydrochloride / Phenyl Hydrazone — carcinogen + blood toxin. Substitute wherever possible.

Sodium Nitroprusside — cyanide complex. NEVER acidify.

Tollen’s Reagent — forms a contact-explosive silver residue if stored. Prepare immediately before use; never store prepared reagent.

Picric Acid — explosive when allowed to dry out. Confirmed currently stored correctly as a saturated solution; verify monthly that it has not evaporated or crystallised.

Malachite Green — not FSSAI-permitted in any food-contact application. Confirm this stock is lab-stain grade only and segregated from any product-contact area.

Trypan Blue — suspected carcinogen/teratogen. Gloves, dedicated waste stream, avoid skin contact.

Cross-reference: OB-REF-CHEM-003 separately flags Potassium Dichromate, Mercuric Sulphate, and Sodium Arsenate as additional Group 1 carcinogens in physical stock — not re-listed here as they did not appear in this particular inventory export; confirm their status independently.
